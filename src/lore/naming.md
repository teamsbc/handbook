# Naming

## TeamSBC

TeamSBC takes its name from the team and the computers the distribution targets. SBC stands for **single-board computer**: a small computer built around a single circuit board, such as a Raspberry Pi or a Radxa board. TeamSBC also provides images for virtual machines.

## Variants

Variant names come from mountains in the Himalayas. **Makalu** is the current variant; see [Variants](../getting-started/variants.md) for its maintenance model.
